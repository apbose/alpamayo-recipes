# Completed 128-clip ablation table

All values are minADE in metres, lower is better. Brackets show percentage
degradation relative to the **same checkpoint's ten-step** result. All evaluations
use 128 fixed validation clips, one window each, six candidates, seed 42 and eager
attention. The six formerly missing cells completed on September 24 in 2 h 30 min.

## Interpretation

- A5 has the lowest measured minADE, but received 1,986 updates instead of
  A2/A3/A4's 662; no matched three-pass flow-only baseline exists.
- A2 and A3 remain close: no robust shortcut-specific improvement is established.
- A6's small five-step percentage regression does not imply better absolute
  accuracy. Its ten-step baseline is worse than A2/A3/A5.
- A6's two-step percentage regression exceeds released-model regression
  (+19.76% versus +18.62%). The absolute increases are similar (0.2426 m versus
  0.2486 m); the smaller A6 baseline changes the denominator.
- A6/A7 is the intended matched target-supervision comparison; A7 has no results.
- These are open-loop, best-of-six metrics, not deployed-policy or safety results.
- Hardware power caps differed during the missing-cell runs. Do not infer latency
  speedups by combining these runs with the earlier host's timing measurements.
